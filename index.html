Artifact 3: AI Brainstorming & Research Using STORM AI

Objective

The objective of this project was to explore how artificial intelligence tools can enhance brainstorming, research, and idea development. Using the STORM AI platform, our team investigated topics related to fitness, mental health, and social science to understand how AI can support data analysis and structured research. The project focused on combining AI-generated suggestions with human evaluation to produce meaningful insights, organize ideas effectively, and identify key patterns. By leveraging AI tools, we aimed to improve efficiency in research, enhance collaborative problem-solving, and demonstrate the practical application of AI in real-world contexts. Overall, this artifact highlights the role of AI in supporting informed decision-making and structured, data-driven research.

Process

1. Topic Selection

Our team selected research topics related to fitness, mental health, and lifestyle habits.

2. AI Brainstorming

STORM AI was used to generate research ideas and identify key themes.

3. Research Analysis

AI insights were compared with external research sources to validate findings.

4. Insight Development

Key patterns and observations were identified from the research.

Tools Used

Tool Purpose
STORM AI Brainstorming and research idea generation
ChatGPT Refining insights and summarizing research
Online Sources Validating and supporting AI-generated insights

  • Exercise improves mental health and reduces stress.
  • AI tools help analyze large amounts of information efficiently.
  • Social environments influence healthy behavior.
  • AI-assisted research can support data-driven decision making.
